Each card carries the route, the component, and the endpoints that page actually calls.

Notes

Four things the cards above do not say on their own.

There is no stage 2

Stage 2 was the account-claim screen, removed on 2026-08-20 along with the §10 relevance signal. The value left the union rather than staying as a stage nothing occupies, and 3–5 deliberately were not renumbered — stage numbers appear in comments across the tree, and closing the gap would put every one of them out of step with the code beside it.

The register's own header is stale

flow.ts opens with “twenty-six were planned and twenty-four are here.” The register now holds 28. The count in the prose went out of date; the array did not, which is why this page counts the array.

One register, three readers

The router, the help drawer, and §33.11's principal-flow register all read FOUNDER_FLOW_PAGES. Three hand-written lists would be two chances to disagree, and the disagreement shows up as a help card that jumps somewhere that does not exist.

Two pages store nothing

invite and reach read the draft landing and write nothing at all. That is load-bearing for reach: it is a recorded deviation, and reading no record and storing nothing is one of the two mechanisms that keep it narrow.
